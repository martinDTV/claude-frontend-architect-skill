# v1.5.0 release candidate: visual review of Atlas, Lumen and Ember (increment 6, 2026-10-31)

Two reviews of the same captures, kept apart because they are not the same thing:

- **The author's review** (section 1): written by the session that built the screens, from the captures under `verification/browser/evidence/review-rc/` (121 PNG files, `index.json` with route, viewport, theme, text scale, overflow and console errors per file; regenerable with `node verification/browser/review-captures.mjs`, which also writes the route audits' JSON). An author cannot be independent of their own choices; this section says what the author saw and changed, with the file names.
- **The fresh-context review** (section 2): a general-purpose agent of the same model family, started without this session's history and given only the directory of captures and the rubric, and told not to assume the screens are good. It is not a human review: it reads pixels, it does not use the product, and it shares the model's blind spots. Its text is reproduced as delivered, without edits.

Neither replaces the human review this candidate still needs before publication: a person at 375 and 1280 px, both themes, motion on and off, reading the copy aloud.

## Independent design and accessibility review — review-rc captures

### Method and caveats

- Reviewed all 121 PNGs plus `index.json` (123 captures + one `detail-path` marker). The index reports **one horizontal overflow** (`lumen-landing-mobile-light-200.png`, captured 396 px wide instead of 375) and **no console errors**.
- **Nuxt captures are viewport-only.** Every `nuxt-app-*` file is exactly 1280×800 / 820×1180 / 375×740 / 320×640 although the index says `fullPage: true`; the Nuxt shell scrolls internally. Anything below the first screen on Nuxt mobile/reflow (card list beyond three cards, pagination, form submit buttons on desktop) could not be inspected. The Next, Lumen and Ember captures are true full pages.
- Full-page captures freeze fixed/sticky elements where they were at capture time: the Next sidebar background stopping at y=800 on taller pages, Lumen's "249 € · Lumen / Pre-order" bar sitting mid-page, Ember's "Add to bag" bar overlapping the thumbnail strip on mobile. These are capture artefacts, not defects, and are excluded below.
- Very tall captures (Lumen mobile 200 % at 14,654 px, Next shipments mobile 200 % at 11,216 px) were only readable at thumbnail scale; findings from them are marked as such.

#### Observations

**CONFIRMED DEFECT**
1. **"Try again" is invisible in the error state, both themes.** `nuxt-app-dashboard-error-desktop-light.png` (y≈167) and `nuxt-app-shipments-error-mobile-dark.png` (y≈350) show the retry control as a ghost of text in almost the same colour as the canvas. The only recovery action in the error path is unreadable. Compare `next-app-dashboard-error-desktop-light.png`, where it is an outlined button.
2. **Field-level validation errors are styled as hints.** In `nuxt-app-shipments-new-errors-desktop-light.png` the messages under Reference, Customer, Promised date, Weight and Pallets ("Use the format ATL-26001…", "Weight must be at least 1 kg.") are the same muted grey as "Must differ from the origin."; inputs have no error border. Only the summary box is red. Same in `nuxt-app-shipments-new-conflict-mobile-dark.png` ("A shipment with reference ATL-26001 already exists." in grey under the field) and in `nuxt-app-shipments-assign-dialog-desktop-light.png`, where the note promises "its validation message" but "Choose a driver who is on duty." appears in default text colour with no error treatment (the Next build renders the same sentence in red with a red field).
3. **Contradictory validation copy.** The same errors capture lists both "Enter the promised date and time." and "The promised time is in the past; pick a later slot." for an empty date field ("Check 6 fields"); the Next build correctly lists 5.
4. **200 % text, desktop: sidebar breaks.** `nuxt-app-dashboard-desktop-light-200.png`: the brand wraps to two lines and the collapse icon is drawn over the word "Atlas"; nav labels truncate to "Da…", "Sh…", "New sh…", "Fl…", "Se…"; the search field loses its label and shows only the ⌘ K keycaps. Primary navigation is unreadable at the WCAG 1.4.4 threshold.
5. **200 % text, mobile: header collision.** `nuxt-app-shipments-mobile-light-200.png`: the "New shipment" button overlaps the hamburger icon and the page title disappears entirely.
6. **Toolbar flush with header on tablet and mobile.** `nuxt-app-shipments-tablet-light.png`, `nuxt-app-shipments-mobile-light.png`: the search input's top edge touches the header's bottom border (desktop has ~8 px).
7. **Chart text does not scale.** `nuxt-app-dashboard-desktop-light-200.png`: axis labels and legend stay at 1× size inside the SVG while everything else doubles.

**DESIGN RECOMMENDATION**
- Give "Late" at least the same weight as "Delivered" (a danger pill), since it is the status dispatchers are scanning for. Both Atlas design pages disagree on this, so settle it in the contract.
- Loading skeleton should mirror the loaded structure (5 cards + 2 panels + table), not two bars.
- On mobile, put the five KPIs in a 2-column grid or a compact strip so the chart and exceptions are reachable without a full screen of scrolling.
- Differentiate "Cancel shipment" in the row menu (red, separated), as the Next build does.
- Settings: Reference and Status are described as always visible ("At least reference and status stay visible") but their checkboxes look fully enabled (`nuxt-app-settings-desktop-light.png`); render them locked like the Next build.
- "Status" select shows placeholder styling while "All lanes" shows a value; pick one convention for the toolbar.

**HUMAN JUDGEMENT**
- Whether 24 px "Details"/"Assign" buttons on mobile cards are acceptable for field use with gloves or on a moving vehicle; they meet 2.5.8 but not 2.5.5.
- Whether the five-card KPI strip is the right first screen on tablet (orphan card) or should become 3 + 2.

**NOT VERIFIABLE FROM CAPTURES**
- Pagination, the end of the form (submit/cancel placement), the mobile card list beyond three cards, and whether the dropped table columns/row actions on tablet are reachable by horizontal scroll (no scrollbar or fade is visible).
- Hover states, focus order, the "cancel shipment" confirmation (the file named `cancel-confirm` contains the details panel).

#### Verdict
**Not ready** as a reference implementation in its current state, though the fixes are small. An invisible recovery action in the error path (1), field errors that cannot be told from hints (2) and a primary navigation that becomes "Da… / Sh…" at 200 % (4) are exactly the categories a design-contract method claims to prevent. Fix those three plus the header collision at mobile 200 % (5) and it moves to "ready with reservations" (chart text scaling, tablet column loss, Late styling).

#### Observations

**CONFIRMED DEFECT**
1. **200 % text, desktop: grid does not drop columns and text overflows cards.** `ember-shop-desktop-light-200.png`: the sidebar keeps its width, the grid stays 4-up with ~60 px product thumbnails, card text wraps one word per line ("Noodle / bowl / Hana / Okada / €69 / In / stock") and "Maja Bergström" crosses the card's right border. The shop is unusable at 200 %.
2. **200 % text, mobile: still 2-up.** `ember-shop-mobile-light-200.png` (thumbnail scale): names wrap to four lines, prices and "In stock" wrap, header nav breaks into "All / pieces", "Last / few", search placeholder shrinks to "Se". Should be a single column.
3. **Mobile product page order.** `ember-detail-mobile-light.png` / `-dark.png`: after the image come "About this piece", "Care", "The maker", and only then maker, title, price, availability, glaze and size pickers. A shopper must scroll past the prose to choose a glaze or size. The sticky bar shows name/price/"Add to bag" but not the variant choice.
4. **Toasts cover the bag drawer's footer.** `ember-bag-drawer-desktop-light.png`: two identical "Added to your bag" toasts (one per unit of quantity 2) stack over the drawer and hide Subtotal/Delivery/Total ("Sub…", "Deli…", "Tota…" visible at the edge) and whatever CTA sits beneath. `ember-bag-drawer-mobile-dark.png`: one toast covers the footer region. A toast is also redundant while the drawer it advertises is already open.
5. **Sticky bar title truncates to one letter.** `ember-detail-reflow-light-200.png`: "W…" next to "€54 · In stock" and "Add to bag".
6. **Sold-out copy promises an alternative that does not exist.** `ember-detail-sold-out-desktop-light.png`: "This glaze and size is gone; another glaze may be in stock." while only one glaze (Shino) and one size (L) are offered.
7. **Tablet header wrap.** `ember-shop-tablet-light.png`: theme and bag buttons wrap to a second row at the far left, under the wordmark, while the search stays on row one at the right.

**DESIGN RECOMMENDATION**
- Dark theme: tint the image stage (e.g. a warm dark grey) instead of keeping the light tile; the current contrast fights the product.
- Desktop detail: make the buy column sticky or cap the image height; compose the object lower on the stage so it sits on the floor line rather than floating above its shadow.
- Mobile header: collapse nav into a menu and the search into an icon; four rows of header before content is a lot.
- Pagination at 320 px shows eleven controls on two rows (`ember-shop-reflow-light.png`); "‹ 1 / 10 ›" would do.
- Bag page and drawer should share one quantity control.
- Remove the duplicated "Filter" heading inside the filter sheet.
- Disable the quantity input and desaturate the button further on sold-out pieces.

**HUMAN JUDGEMENT**
- Whether the faceted-polygon vessels are an acceptable stand-in for product photography in a reference build, or whether they undercut the "named makers, real stock" premise.
- Whether the "Add to bag" sticky bar on mobile is enough to compensate for the ordering in defect 3 (I don't think it is, because variant choice is the decision).

**NOT VERIFIABLE FROM CAPTURES**
- Focus visibility anywhere in the shop (no focused element captured); glaze switch animation; whether the drawer has a checkout CTA under the toasts; keyboard reachability of the filter sheet.

#### Verdict
**Not ready.** The states and filtering model are thoughtful and the no-match/empty/last-few/made-to-order handling is better than most real shops, but three defects sit on the core shopping path: the grid collapses at 200 % (1, 2), the mobile product page buries price and variant pickers under the prose (3), and toasts hide the bag totals (4). Fix those and tone the dark-theme tiles, and it is a solid "ready with reservations"; the artwork remains a human call.
