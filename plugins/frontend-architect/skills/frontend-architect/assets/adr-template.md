# NNNN. Decision title

- Status: proposed | accepted | superseded by [NNNN](NNNN-title.md)
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Deciders: names or roles

## Context

The problem and the forces at play: product needs, team, existing code, constraints. Facts, not opinions.

## Requirements

The requirements this decision must satisfy (functional and non-functional: SEO, performance budget, security, team autonomy, deadlines).

## Alternatives considered

1. **Option A**: one line. Pros. Cons.
2. **Option B**: one line. Pros. Cons.
3. **Keep the current approach**: always consider it.

## Decision

The chosen option in one or two sentences.

## Justification

Why it wins against the requirements; which trade-offs were accepted.

## Consequences

What becomes easier; what becomes harder; migration or follow-up work.

## Risks

What could go wrong and how it is mitigated.

## Review triggers

Measurable conditions that should reopen this decision (for example: "a second team starts shipping to the dashboard weekly", "LCP p75 above 2.5 s for a month", "the library has no release for 12 months").
